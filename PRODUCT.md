# CargoOS product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript and Vite. The website uses synthetic records and browser-local persistence for the website-only scope.

## Users

Booking, dispatch, receiving, delivery and accounts staff at a multi-branch cargo operator. Managers monitor cargo movement and branch performance.

## Product purpose

Move a consignment from a counter booking through trip allotment, dispatch, acceptance, delivery and payment. Use the LR number as the common reference across workflows.

## Evidence on hand

The two local screenshot archives contain 41 unique screens. They establish the shape of legacy workflows. The user selected an original CargoOS visual design for those workflows.
